  • Cosmic-Time Generative Presentism (CTGP) proposes that only the currently generated state of the universe exists. Earlier states genuinely existed when they were present but no longer exist. Generation is gapless: reality never lapses, and each present state arises from immediately preceding states through lawful temporal and generative progression. What ceases to exist is each earlier state itself, not necessarily the physical systems that were in that state; whether those systems persist depends on their own conditions of persistence. Gapless generation does not require every physical quantity to change smoothly; abrupt changes, including quantum jumps in models that contain them, are compatible with it. The physical consequences of what previously occurred are instantiated in presently existing configurations of matter, fields, and radiation, some of which constitute present records of past events. These consequences and records are not the past itself; they are parts of the currently existing physical reality. The past consists of what actually happened during formerly present stages, while the future has not yet been generated.
